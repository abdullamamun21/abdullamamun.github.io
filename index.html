About Me

Dr. Abdulla Al Mamun is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the School of Earth Science and Engineering, Hohai University, Nanjing, China. He holds a Ph.D. in Hydrology and Water Resources from Hohai University, two M.Sc. degrees in Mathematics, and a B.Sc. (Hons) in Mathematics.

His research focuses on analytical and numerical methods for fractional PDEs, nonlinear wave models, soliton dynamics, bifurcation, stability analysis, and mathematical modeling of fluid dynamics and water-wave phenomena.
