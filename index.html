About

Dr. Abdulla Al Mamun is a Postdoctoral Fellow at Hohai University, Nanjing, China. His research focuses on applied and computational mathematics, fractional partial differential equations, nonlinear waves, soliton theory, numerical analysis, scientific computing, and hydrological modeling.
